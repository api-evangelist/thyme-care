Summary: Nashville Post reported that Nashville-based Thyme Care named Brad Diephuis chief executive officer, with co-founder Robin Shah moving to executive chairman to lead work on drug affordability, clinical trial access, and care alignment, both effective September 1. Thyme Care added three health plan partners in the first half of 2026, and its platform is available to 10.5 million Americans across all 50 states, managing $6 billion of oncology spend. Nashville-based oncology company Thyme Care has named Brad Diephuis as chief executive officer.
